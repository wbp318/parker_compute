## Step 0 — Decide registered agent + business address

**Registered agent** is the human or company that accepts legal mail (lawsuits, state notices) on behalf of the LLC. In Louisiana, they must:
- Be a Louisiana resident OR a registered LA business
- Have a physical street address in LA (no PO boxes)
- Be available during normal business hours

**Your options:**
- **Be your own** — free, but your home address goes on the public state record. Anyone can search it on the SOS site.
- **Use a registered agent service** — Northwest, Harbor Compliance, ZenBusiness, etc. ~$100–150/year. Keeps your home address private.

**Recommendation:** for $125/year, use a registered agent service. Privacy is worth it. If you're cash-conservative for year one, be your own — you can switch later via Form 977 (~$25).

**Business address question:** even if you use a registered agent, the LLC needs a *principal business address.* In Louisiana you can use the same address as the registered agent in most cases. If you use your home, accept that it becomes public.

- [ ] Decide: be own agent OR pay a service (recommended)
- [ ] Decide: principal business address (home / RA service / PO Box / virtual mailbox)

## Step 2 — File Articles of Organization

This is the moment the LLC legally exists.

- [ ] Create a GeauxBiz account at https://geauxbiz.sos.la.gov
- [ ] File **Articles of Organization for a Louisiana Limited Liability Company** (Form 365)
- [ ] **Fee: $105** (online) — paper filing is $100 but adds a week. Pay the $5.
- [ ] Optional expedite: **$30** for 24-hour processing, or **$50** for same-day-while-you-wait. For most cases the standard 1–2 business day online turnaround is fine.
- [ ] You'll also file an **Initial Report** at the same time (lists managers/members and registered agent). No extra fee when bundled with Articles.

**What goes in the form:**
- LLC name: `Parker AI Works, L.L.C.` (Louisiana requires "L.L.C." or "Limited Liability Company")
- Purpose: a broad phrase like *"to engage in any lawful activity for which a Louisiana limited liability company may be organized."* This keeps you flexible.
- Registered agent name + LA address
- Members and managers (you, as sole member and manager)
- Duration: perpetual (default)
- Effective date: immediate, or you can pick a future date up to 5 days out

- [ ] Save the **Certificate of Organization** PDF when it comes back. You'll need it for the bank.

## Step 3 — Operating Agreement

**Required?** No, Louisiana doesn't require one for a single-member LLC. **Do it anyway.** Reasons:
- Banks may ask to see one
- It strengthens the liability shield (without it, a court can argue your LLC and you are functionally indistinguishable)
- Future investors / partners will demand one
- It's free if you draft it yourself

**Sections to include (single-member version):**
1. Formation (name, date, state)
2. Member (you, 100% ownership)
3. Management (member-managed; you as sole manager)
4. Capital contribution (initial cash put in — even $100 is fine)
5. Profits/losses/distributions (all to you)
6. Bookkeeping & tax treatment (default: disregarded entity, taxed on your personal 1040 Schedule C)
7. Transfer / dissolution (what happens if you die, sell, or wind down)
8. Indemnification of the manager

- [ ] Draft Operating Agreement (template at the end of this doc — copy to a new file, fill in blanks)
- [ ] Sign, date, store with the Certificate of Organization. **You do not file this with the state.** It's an internal document.

## Step 4 — EIN (Employer Identification Number)

This is the LLC's federal tax ID. Free, takes 10 minutes.

- [ ] Go to https://www.irs.gov/ein
- [ ] Apply online (Monday–Friday, 7am–10pm ET — it's a real-time system)
- [ ] Single-member LLC, taxed as disregarded entity (sole proprietor)
- [ ] Save the **CP 575 letter** as PDF — banks ask for this

## Step 5 — East Carroll Parish occupational license

Louisiana has no statewide business license, but parishes and municipalities require one. East Carroll Parish handles licensing through the Police Jury.

- [ ] **Call the East Carroll Parish Police Jury** at **318-559-1618** (office at 409 2nd St., Suite 6, Lake Providence, LA 71254)
- [ ] Ask: *"I'm forming an LLC for an AI / web consulting business operating from my home in [your town]. What occupational license do I need, what does it cost, and what forms do you need from me?"*
- [ ] Most LA parishes charge **$50–$200/year** for service-business licenses
- [ ] Some parishes also require **municipal** licensing if you're inside an incorporated town's limits — ask about that on the same call
- [ ] Bring: Certificate of Organization, EIN letter, ID

This is also the right call to ask about any zoning rules for a home-based business. Probably none in your area, but verify.

## Step 6 — State tax accounts

Most consulting services are not subject to Louisiana sales tax. But you still register with the Louisiana Department of Revenue to file income tax under the LLC.

- [ ] Register at the Louisiana Taxpayer Access Point (LaTAP): https://latap.revenue.louisiana.gov
- [ ] Single-member LLC defaults to "disregarded entity" — you'll file as a sole proprietor (Schedule C) on your personal return. No separate state return needed at the LLC level unless you elect to be taxed as a corporation (don't, for now).
- [ ] If you ever sell **digital products** (a prompt template PDF for $19, an online course, etc.), revisit sales tax — those *are* often taxable.

## Step 7 — Bank account

You must keep business and personal finances separate. This is what actually preserves the liability shield in court.

- [ ] Decide: local bank (relationship, in-person service) or online bank (better software)
  - Local options: Bank of Oak Ridge, Origin Bank, Cross Keys Bank (all have NE LA branches)
  - Online options: **Mercury** (free, great for tech companies), **Relay** (free, designed for small biz), Bluevine
- [ ] What to bring:
  - Certificate of Organization
  - EIN letter (CP 575)
  - Operating Agreement
  - Government ID
  - Opening deposit (typically $25–$100)
- [ ] Get a debit card and check book
- [ ] **Move all business income and expenses through this account from day one.** No mixing.

## Step 8 — Bookkeeping

Set this up *before* your first invoice. Reconstructing books later is misery.

- [ ] Pick one:
  - **Wave** — free, simple, good for solos. Best for now.
  - **QuickBooks Online** — $30/mo, more powerful, what your accountant probably wants. Switch to this when you have 5+ clients.
- [ ] Link the business bank account
- [ ] Create categories: Revenue (Audit / Workflow Pack / Training / Retainer), Expenses (Software, Marketing, Insurance, Travel, Professional Services, Phone, Internet)
- [ ] Save every receipt. Apps that auto-capture: Wave, Expensify (free tier)

---

## Step 9 — Insurance

Don't skip. Don't delay. Get quotes the week you file the LLC.

- [ ] **Professional Liability / Errors & Omissions (E&O)** — protects you when a client claims your work caused them harm. $1M aggregate is standard.
  - Get quotes from **Hiscox**, **Next Insurance**, **Thimble** — all online, ~10 minutes each
  - Expect **$40–80/month** for a solo consultant. Lock in before client #1.
- [ ] **General Liability** — protects against bodily injury / property damage. Required if you ever host clients at your office or do on-site workshops. ~$25/month. Optional for now.
- [ ] **Cyber Liability** — required by most law firms and healthcare clients. Add when you start that vertical, not before. ~$30–50/month.

## Step 10 — Annual maintenance (year 2 onward)

- [ ] **File Annual Report** with LA SOS by the anniversary of your formation date. **$30 online**, $35 by mail.
- [ ] **Renew East Carroll Parish occupational license** (yearly, usually January renewal)
- [ ] **Renew domain** ($12)
- [ ] **Renew E&O insurance** (annual policy)
- [ ] **File taxes** — LLC income flows to your personal 1040 (Schedule C). Hire a CPA in year one — costs $300–800 and is worth it.

## Templates and forms

### Operating Agreement — single-member, minimal template

> Don't run this past a client without an attorney review. This is a *starting point*, not a final document. Budget $500 with a small-biz attorney in Monroe, Shreveport, or Baton Rouge for a one-hour review pass.

```
OPERATING AGREEMENT OF PARKER AI WORKS, L.L.C.
A Single-Member Louisiana Limited Liability Company

This Operating Agreement is entered into as of [DATE] by the sole
member of Parker AI Works, L.L.C. (the "Company"), a Louisiana
limited liability company.

1. FORMATION
   1.1 The Company was formed on [DATE] by filing Articles of
       Organization with the Louisiana Secretary of State.
   1.2 The Company's principal place of business is
       [ADDRESS], East Carroll Parish, Louisiana.

2. SOLE MEMBER
   2.1 The sole member of the Company is Tap Parker
       ("Member"), who holds a 100% membership interest.

3. MANAGEMENT
   3.1 The Company is member-managed. The Member has full and
       exclusive authority to manage and operate the Company.

4. CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION
   4.1 The Member has contributed [$AMOUNT] to the Company as
       initial capital.
   4.2 The Member is not required to make additional contributions
       except as the Member, in their sole discretion, determines.

5. DISTRIBUTIONS
   5.1 All profits and losses of the Company are allocated to the
       Member. Distributions may be made at any time at the
       Member's discretion.

6. TAX TREATMENT
   6.1 The Company elects to be treated as a disregarded entity
       for U.S. federal income tax purposes. The Member shall
       report all income and loss on the Member's personal
       federal income tax return.

7. BOOKS AND RECORDS
   7.1 The Member shall maintain accurate books and records of
       all Company financial activities, kept separate from
       personal accounts.

8. INDEMNIFICATION
   8.1 The Company shall indemnify the Member to the fullest
       extent permitted by Louisiana law for actions taken in
       good faith on behalf of the Company.

9. TRANSFER OF INTEREST
   9.1 The Member may not transfer the Membership Interest
       except as permitted by Louisiana law and subject to any
       required filings.

10. DISSOLUTION
    10.1 The Company shall dissolve upon the Member's written
         election, the Member's death (subject to estate law),
         or as required by Louisiana law.

11. AMENDMENT
    11.1 This Agreement may be amended in writing signed by the
         Member.

12. GOVERNING LAW
    12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
         Louisiana.

SIGNED:

____________________________________   Date: _______________
Tap Parker, Sole Member
```
