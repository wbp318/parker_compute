## The two-document pattern

Most professional services firms use a **two-tier contract structure**:

1. **MSA — Master Services Agreement.** Signed once per client, at the start of the relationship. Covers the rules of the road: payment terms, IP, confidentiality, liability, dispute resolution. **It does not, by itself, commit either party to any specific project.**
2. **SOW — Statement of Work.** Signed for each engagement. Defines the actual deliverables, timeline, and fees for one specific project. Each SOW is governed by the MSA.

Why two documents instead of one? Because once the MSA is signed, follow-on projects only need a new SOW — a one-page document — instead of re-negotiating the whole contract. Saves enormous time once a client converts to a second or third engagement.

## Workflow

1. Take both templates to a Louisiana attorney for review. Make their edits.
2. Save the final versions as `MSA_v1.md` and `SOW_v1.md` (or `.docx` after conversion).
3. For each new client:
   - Fill in MSA blanks → send for signature → file the signed PDF.
   - For each engagement under that client, fill in a fresh SOW → send for signature → file the signed PDF.
4. Re-review with the attorney **once a year** or whenever you take on a new vertical (legal, healthcare, etc.) that has its own rules.

## What's NOT in these templates

- **NDA-only relationships.** If a client wants just an NDA before disclosing anything, use a one-page mutual NDA template, then move to the MSA when scope is clear. Get an NDA template from your attorney.
- **Equity / revenue share deals.** Don't take these as a beginner. If you must, the structure goes in the SOW, not the MSA.
- **Reseller / channel partner agreements.** Different document entirely.
- **Independent contractor agreements for hiring help.** When you eventually subcontract another consultant, you sign a *separate* contract with them — not derived from these templates.

## Things to remember every time you send one

- Send PDFs, not Word docs. Use DocuSign, HelloSign, or Adobe Sign for e-signature. **Free for low volume.**
- Always send the MSA + the first SOW *together* in the first engagement. Two signatures in one transaction. Don't sign one and "do the SOW later."
- Keep a signed PDF of every contract in a folder organized by client name. Backup to cloud storage.
- When you raise prices, draft a *new* SOW reflecting the new price — never amend the MSA just for pricing.
- If a client lawyer asks for redlines, accept changes you can live with (e.g. payment Net 30 instead of Net 15), push back hard on changes that shift risk to you (e.g. unlimited liability, broad indemnification, IP assignment of pre-existing work).
