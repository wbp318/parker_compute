**Parker AI Works, L.L.C.**, a Louisiana limited liability company with its principal place of business at [ADDRESS], East Carroll Parish, Louisiana ("**Consultant**"); and

## 1. Services

1.1 **Statements of Work.** Consultant will provide professional consulting services to Client as described in one or more Statements of Work signed by both Parties ("**SOWs**"). Each SOW is governed by this Agreement. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and a SOW, this Agreement controls *except* for terms specifically negotiated in the SOW.

1.2 **No exclusivity.** This Agreement is non-exclusive. Either Party may engage with other parties, including competitors, subject to confidentiality obligations in Section 6.

1.3 **No obligation to perform without a SOW.** Consultant has no obligation to perform any services, and Client has no obligation to pay any fees, except as specified in an executed SOW.

---

## 2. Fees and Payment

2.1 **Fees.** Fees for each engagement are set forth in the applicable SOW. All fees are quoted in U.S. dollars and exclude any applicable sales, use, or value-added tax, which is Client's responsibility.

2.2 **Invoicing.** Consultant will invoice Client according to the schedule in the SOW. Default schedule, unless otherwise stated: 50% on signing of the SOW, 50% on delivery.

2.3 **Payment terms.** Payment is due **Net 15** from the invoice date. Payment shall be made by ACH, wire transfer, or check to an account specified by Consultant.

2.4 **Late payment.** Any amount not paid within 15 days of the invoice date accrues interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by Louisiana law. Consultant may suspend performance, including but not limited to refusing to deliver work product, until past-due amounts are paid in full. Consultant may also recover reasonable attorney's fees and collection costs.

2.5 **Expenses.** Out-of-pocket expenses (travel, third-party tool licenses purchased specifically for Client) require Client's prior written approval and are reimbursable at cost.

---

## 3. Term and Termination

3.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section 3.

3.2 **Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any specific SOW, on **thirty (30) days written notice** to the other Party.

3.3 **Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW immediately upon written notice if the other Party:
 (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 15 days of receiving written notice; or
 (b) becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or has an involuntary bankruptcy proceeding filed against it.

3.4 **Effect of termination.** Upon termination of an SOW:
 (a) Client will pay Consultant for all services performed and expenses incurred up to the effective date of termination, plus any non-refundable fees and a reasonable wind-down fee;
 (b) Consultant will deliver to Client any work product Client has paid for in full;
 (c) Each Party will return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information, subject to legal retention obligations.

3.5 **Survival.** Sections 2 (with respect to amounts already accrued), 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, and 14 survive any termination of this Agreement.

## 4. Independent Contractor

Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, agency, or franchise between the Parties. Consultant is responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on amounts received under this Agreement. Consultant is not entitled to benefits offered to Client's employees.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria and Fix-It Window

5.1 **Acceptance.** Each SOW specifies its deliverables. A deliverable is deemed accepted when Client either (a) provides written acceptance, or (b) does not provide written notice of rejection within **seven (7) business days** of delivery.

5.2 **Rejection.** A written notice of rejection must specify in reasonable detail how the deliverable fails to meet the acceptance criteria stated in the SOW.

5.3 **Fix-it window.** For thirty (30) days following acceptance of the final deliverable in an SOW, Consultant will, at no additional charge, correct material defects in the delivered work that prevent it from functioning substantially as described in the SOW. **This fix-it window does not cover new feature requests, changes in scope, or modifications driven by changes to third-party tools or platforms** — those are handled via Change Order under Section 5.4.

5.4 **Change orders.** Any change to the scope, fees, or timeline of an SOW must be documented in a written change order signed by both Parties. Consultant has no obligation to perform out-of-scope work until a change order is signed.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed by one Party ("**Disclosing Party**") to the other ("**Receiving Party**") that is marked confidential, identified as confidential at the time of disclosure, or would reasonably be understood as confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes customer lists, financial information, technical methodologies, source code, business plans, prompt libraries, and personally identifiable information of third parties.

6.2 **Obligations.** The Receiving Party shall:
 (a) use Confidential Information solely to perform its obligations under this Agreement;
 (b) protect Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care;
 (c) limit disclosure to personnel and authorized subcontractors with a need to know who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 6;
 (d) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent.

6.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:
 (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;
 (b) was known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure without confidentiality obligations;
 (c) is independently developed without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
 (d) is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations.

6.4 **Compelled disclosure.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information if compelled by court order, subpoena, or applicable law, provided that the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice (where legally permitted) and reasonable cooperation to seek a protective order.

6.5 **Term.** Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for **three (3) years**, except that trade secrets are protected for so long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

## 7. Data Handling and AI Tool Usage

7.1 **AI tools.** Consultant uses third-party generative AI services (including but not limited to Anthropic Claude and OpenAI ChatGPT). When handling Client Confidential Information or any personal data of Client's customers, employees, or patients, Consultant will use only enterprise-tier AI services (e.g., Claude for Work, ChatGPT Enterprise, Microsoft Copilot for M365) that contractually prohibit use of inputs for model training and provide configurable retention.

7.2 **Prohibited inputs.** Consultant will not input the following into any AI service unless expressly authorized in writing by Client:
 (a) Protected Health Information as defined by HIPAA;
 (b) cardholder data subject to PCI DSS;
 (c) attorney-client privileged communications;
 (d) trade secrets or source code marked "Highly Confidential" by Client;
 (e) personal data of identifiable individuals other than what is reasonably necessary for the engagement.

7.3 **Data location and retention.** Consultant will, at Client's request, document the AI service tier used, the data retention setting, and the data residency for each tool used during the engagement.

7.4 **Hallucinations and verification.** Client acknowledges that all generative AI output may contain factual errors, fabricated citations, or other inaccuracies. Consultant's deliverables include documentation of how to verify outputs before relying on them. **Client is solely responsible for verifying AI outputs before using them in any decision affecting Client's customers, employees, finances, or legal position.**

7.5 **Subprocessors.** Consultant will not use subcontractors who have access to Client Confidential Information without Client's prior written consent. Consultant remains responsible for any subprocessor's compliance with this Agreement.

## 8. Intellectual Property

8.1 **Pre-existing IP.** Each Party retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing intellectual property. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of pre-existing IP.

8.2 **Consultant tools and methodologies.** Consultant retains all rights to its general methodologies, frameworks, prompt-engineering techniques, templates, internal tools, training materials, and know-how, whether developed before or during this engagement. None of these are work-for-hire.

8.3 **Deliverables.** Upon Client's payment in full of the fees due for a deliverable under an SOW, Consultant assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in the **specific deliverable** as listed in the SOW (e.g., a specific Claude Project configured for Client, a specific written report, a specific PDF user guide). This assignment **excludes** Consultant's pre-existing IP, methodologies, and tools that may be incorporated into the deliverable; for those, Consultant grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use them solely as embedded in the delivered work product.

8.4 **Client data.** Client retains all right, title, and interest in Client's data and Confidential Information. Consultant has no ownership interest in Client data.

8.5 **Right to reference.** Consultant may reference the existence of the engagement, Client's name, and Client's industry in marketing materials (e.g., a "case study" or client list), in **anonymized form** unless Client provides written approval to use its name. Anything more specific than industry-and-engagement-type requires Client's written approval.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

10.1 **Cap on damages.** Except for the Parties' obligations under Section 6 (Confidentiality), Section 7 (Data Handling), and Section 11 (Indemnification), and except for either Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, **each Party's total cumulative liability arising out of or related to this Agreement is limited to the amount of fees actually paid by Client to Consultant under the applicable SOW in the six (6) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.**

10.2 **No consequential damages.** IN NO EVENT IS EITHER PARTY LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, PUNITIVE, OR EXEMPLARY DAMAGES, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, LOST DATA, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

## 11. Indemnification

11.1 **By Consultant.** Consultant will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Client from third-party claims that the deliverables, **as delivered by Consultant and used in accordance with the SOW**, infringe a U.S. patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret of the third party. Consultant's obligations under this Section 11.1 are conditioned on Client (a) promptly notifying Consultant in writing, (b) granting Consultant sole control of the defense and settlement, and (c) reasonably cooperating.

11.2 **By Client.** Client will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Consultant from third-party claims arising out of (a) Client's use of any deliverable in a manner not authorized under the SOW, (b) Client's data or content provided to Consultant, (c) Client's modifications to deliverables, or (d) Client's combination of deliverables with other products or services not provided by Consultant.

11.3 **Exclusive remedy.** Section 11 states each Party's sole and exclusive remedy, and the other Party's sole and exclusive liability, for third-party claims of the type described above.

## 14. General Provisions

14.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered to the addresses listed in the preamble (or to a more current address provided by the receiving Party in writing). Notice is effective upon (a) personal delivery, (b) confirmed email receipt, or (c) the third business day after deposit in the U.S. mail, certified, return receipt requested.

14.2 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, together with all SOWs executed under it, is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, written or oral.

14.3 **Amendments.** No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

14.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision will be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable while preserving the Parties' original intent.

14.5 **Waiver.** No waiver of any breach is a waiver of any subsequent breach. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

14.6 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except that either Party may assign to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets, provided the successor agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement.

14.7 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, natural disasters, war, terrorism, civil unrest, government action, labor disputes, pandemics, or failure of third-party infrastructure or services.

14.8 **Counterparts; electronic signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts. Electronic signatures (via DocuSign, HelloSign, or similar service) are valid and binding.
